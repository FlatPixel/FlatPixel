Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

Flat Pixel Society SAS (“Flat Pixel Society”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates websites, games, mobile applications, and provides services to business clients (collectively, the “Service”).

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, processed, and protected depending on the nature of the Service provided.

1. Definitions

Personal Data
Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

Client Data
Personal Data processed by Flat Pixel Society on behalf of a business client in the course of providing contracted services.

Consumer Data
Personal Data collected in connection with Flat Pixel Society’s consumer-facing games, websites, or applications.

Data Controller
The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.

Data Processor
The entity that processes personal data on behalf of a Data Controller.

2. Roles and Scope

Client Data

When processing Client Data, Flat Pixel Society acts solely as a data processor and processes such data only in accordance with client instructions and applicable agreements.

Consumer Data

For consumer-facing products and services, Flat Pixel Society acts as a data controller.

The sections below clearly distinguish between these two contexts.

3. Processing of Client Data

Purpose Limitation

Client Data is processed exclusively for the purpose of providing the contracted services to the client. Flat Pixel Society does not use Client Data for product development, analytics, advertising, benchmarking, or any other secondary purpose unless explicitly authorized in writing by the client.

Data Combination

Client Data is not combined with data from other clients, third parties, or Flat Pixel Society’s consumer products.

Data Sharing and Sub-processors

Flat Pixel Society may engage sub-processors solely to provide the contracted services. Any sub-processor:

  • Is bound by written data protection obligations
  • Processes Client Data only on documented client instructions
  • Is not permitted to use Client Data for any independent purpose

Client authorization is required where applicable under contract or law.

International Transfers

Client Data is processed in the geographic regions agreed upon with the client. Transfers to other jurisdictions occur only where necessary to deliver the services and in compliance with applicable data protection laws and contractual safeguards.

Data Retention

Client Data is retained only for the duration of the client relationship, unless a longer retention period is required by law or expressly agreed in writing. Upon termination of the relationship, Client Data is securely deleted or returned according to client instructions.

Security

Flat Pixel Society implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Client Data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Processing of Consumer Data

This section applies only to Flat Pixel Society’s consumer-facing games, applications, and websites.

Types of Consumer Data Collected

  • Usage data (e.g. IP address, device type, session duration)
  • Cookies and similar technologies (You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent)
  • Diagnostic and crash data

Purpose of Processing

Consumer Data is processed to:

  • Operate and maintain consumer products
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve functionality and performance
  • Detect and prevent technical issues

Analytics and Advertising

Analytics and advertising technologies may be used only in consumer-facing products and do not apply to Client Data.

5. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For Consumer Data processed within the EEA, legal bases may include:

  • Performance of a contract
  • Legitimate interests
  • User consent
  • Legal obligations

Client Data is processed under Article 28 GDPR as a data processor.

6. Data Subject Rights

Where applicable, individuals have the right to:

  • Access, rectify, or delete their personal data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable)

To exercise these rights regarding your Consumer Data, please contact us at hello@flatpixel.fr. We may verify your identity before processing your request.

Requests relating to Client Data should be directed to the relevant client (data controller), unless otherwise agreed.

7. Policy Changes

Material changes to this Privacy Policy that affect the processing of Client Data will not apply to existing client engagements without prior notice and, where required, client agreement.

Changes relating solely to consumer-facing services will be communicated via updated postings on our websites or applications.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data protection practices, please contact:
